=== GAME INSTRUCTIONS ===
In this simplified game, you own a business that makes money by selling widgets for 25 simulated days.

* Deciding Order Quantity: At the beginning of each simulated day you have to carefully determine how many widgets to have available for sale in your store that day.
* -1 Per Unit Ordered: For every widget ordered, you pay 1 play dollar and place the widgets in your store. However, you receive 2 dollars at the end of each day for each sold widget. If you cannot afford to pay for the widgets you order you will be BANKRUPT and unable to order more widgets. If this happens, the experiment will end in FAILURE.
* Random Demand: Then roll the 3 dice to determine demand for that day.
* Start Empty for Next Day: Then discard all leftover units from your store and start empty for the beginning of the next day. Any widgets you failed to sell because you over estimated demand will be wasted and you will have paid for them.

You begin with 100 play dollars. Your goal is to maximize your play dollars by the end of the 25 days. This means you cannot over order widgets or you could potentially end up with less dollars than you started with.

The demand each day is the sum of three standard six-sided dice. The dice are rolled for you after you place your order. Your order each day can be any whole number from 3 to 18.
